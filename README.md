<h3>pseudo harem</h3>
<h5>Romance, Comedy</h5>
<p>Eiji Kitahama, a second-year high school student, yearns for popularity more than anything. His days are filled with dreams of being surrounded by admiring peers. Rin Nanakura, his junior in the drama club, decides to use her acting skills to help Eiji achieve this goal. With her talent, she crafts a harem of loving girls, each one showering Eiji with affection. But behind these diverse characters is Rin herself, and her feelings for Eiji are genuine, though Eiji remains completely unaware.

As Eiji receives attention from the various "girls", he is enchanted by Rin's performances. Her colorful and dynamic acting brings joy to his life, leaving him wondering if the actress behind these roles will ever find a way into his heart. The line between reality and performance blurs, creating a drama of its own in which Eiji might discover that the most genuine love comes from the one person who had been there all along.</p>
